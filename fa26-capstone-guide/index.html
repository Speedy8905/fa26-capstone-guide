Ned's Capstone Survival Guide

This capstone is a fictional learning exercise. It uses public information about a real game, but it does not represent work done for, requested by or endorsed by Flood-It, LabPixies, Google or any other company named here. The engagement, the agency, the client contact and the budget are invented for teaching purposes.

This guide is a reference only. It saves nothing, so copy anything you want to keep into your pod's documents. The FA26 Capstone Brief is the source of truth if anything here seems unclear.

Stuck?

Ask your captain first. Still stuck? Bring it to the whole cohort GChat or a senior captain. Captains help with logistics and with navigating the work. They never write the work or give you the answer.
